About Winter Waves

Welcome to Winter Waves, a peaceful getaway where you can leave behind the rush of everyday life and enjoy your time at your own pace. Located in the beautiful coastal surroundings of Goa, Winter Waves is a place where you can relax, spend time with the people you love, and simply enjoy being away from the ordinary. Whether you are planning a family stay, a trip with friends, or a quiet escape, our guest house is designed to give you a comfortable and homely experience. With spacious rooms, a cosy living area, a kitchen where you can prepare your own favourite meals, and a balcony where you can enjoy the surroundings, you have the freedom to make your stay exactly the way you want it. You can cook your own food, enjoy a relaxed meal together, spend the afternoon talking and resting, or simply take some time for yourself. As the sun goes down, Winter Waves becomes even more special. The peaceful atmosphere and beautiful night view create the perfect setting to sit outside, enjoy the evening air, look at the night sky, and forget about the busy world for a while. We want every guest to feel comfortable from the moment they arrive and leave with memories of peaceful mornings, relaxing evenings, good food, and time well spent. Come stay at Winter Waves, make yourself at home, and enjoy a little escape by the sea.
